\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Long- and Ultra-Long Video Understanding}
\label{sec:related-long-video}

Long video understanding needs to balance temporal coverage against the cost of keeping detailed visual evidence. One line of work extends the video context available to multimodal models through compressed representations. MovieChat converts dense video tokens into sparse memory~\cite{song2024moviechat}, while LongVU reduces temporal and spatial redundancy through adaptive compression~\cite{shen2025longvu}. Video-XL uses visual summarization for hour-scale inputs~\cite{shu2025videoxl}, and VideoChat-Flash compresses video tokens hierarchically at the clip and video levels~\cite{li2026videochatflash}. These approaches lower the cost of representing long videos within a limited context budget, but the whole video still has to fit into the model at once. Complementing this model-centric direction, EgoMemReason evaluates reasoning over week-long egocentric recordings and stresses the retrieval and integration of temporally distant evidence~\cite{wang2026egomemreason}.

Another line of work separates video storage from question-time reasoning. DrVideo represents videos as text documents and iteratively retrieves and augments relevant evidence~\cite{ma2025drvideo}. M3-Agent organizes audiovisual observations into entity-centric episodic and semantic memories~\cite{long2026m3agent}, and WorldMM adaptively retrieves from episodic, semantic, and visual memories at multiple temporal granularities~\cite{yeo2026worldmm}. More recently, MERIT builds a multi-key episodic index and expands the temporal context around retrieved segments at inference time~\cite{choi2026merit}. These methods make memory organization and selective evidence access central to long video reasoning, and they complement approaches that mainly compress visual context.

Agentic methods make evidence acquisition interactive. Ego-R1 uses supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning to train a controller that calls hierarchical retrieval and visual perception tools for ultra-long egocentric video reasoning~\cite{tian2026egor1}. VideoPro adaptively chooses between direct video-language reasoning and executable programs, and refines program-based reasoning with execution feedback~\cite{li2026videopro}. Structured querying also has direct precedents: DoraemonGPT builds symbolic video memories that are queried through SQL-generating tools~\cite{yang2024doraemongpt}, while VideoAgent combines temporal memory with an object-memory database accessed through agent-generated SQL~\cite{fan2025videoagent}. Together, these works establish executable reasoning and structured memory access as existing components of video understanding agents.

VideoSQL builds on these memory and agentic approaches by combining SQL-based access to video-derived indices with trainable, reusable skills. Our focus is on improving and reusing the procedures for querying and combining indexed evidence across questions in ultra-long video understanding, rather than on introducing structured video memory or SQL querying on their own.
