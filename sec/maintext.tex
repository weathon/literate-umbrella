\subsection{Datasets}
We evaluated our method on the two most popular datasets in ULVU: EgoLifeQA \cite{yang_egolife_2026} and MM-Lifelong \cite{chen_multimodal_2026}. EgoLifeQA contains multiple-choice questions, and we argue that this does not mimic realistic usage. We find that agents could brute-force verify each option and artificially boost the accuracy.
